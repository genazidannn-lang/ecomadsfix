What changed on Shopify

Shopify retired the old Additional scripts box on the Thank you and Order status pages (for Plus stores in 2025, for other plans in 2026). Tracking now runs through apps and Settings → Customer events, where pixels run in a sandbox. Many stores that "always had tracking" lost their purchase tag in that switch without noticing.

First, look at the status in Google Ads

Open Goals → Conversions → Summary. For each purchase action note:

  • Status: active, no recent conversions, inactive, unverified. Inactive or no recent conversions on the main purchase action means nothing is arriving.
  • Source: is it the Google & YouTube app, Google Tag Manager, an import from GA4, or something else?
  • Primary or secondary: only one purchase action should be Primary, otherwise bidding counts the same order twice.

Zero purchases in Google Ads

  • The Google & YouTube app is connected to a different Google Ads account than the one you look at. Check the account ID in the app settings.
  • The old purchase tag lived in Additional scripts and stopped when Shopify retired it. Nothing replaced it.
  • In Customer events the pixel is disconnected or has no permission to run.
  • Consent: in the EEA and UK, with a cookie banner and no consent mode set up, tags don't fire for visitors who don't accept. Set up consent mode so Google can at least model the missing conversions.
  • The campaign optimizes for a different action than the one actually recording.

Doubled purchases

Almost always two sources counting the same order: the Google & YouTube app and a purchase tag in GTM, or the app plus a GA4 import. Pick one source for the Primary purchase action, set the others to Secondary or remove them. The counting setting is not the culprit: Every is the right choice for purchases, and switching to One would only hide repeat orders.

Wrong values

  • The value includes shipping and tax in one setup and excludes it in another. Decide what ROAS should be based on and use the same everywhere.
  • Currency: with Shopify Markets the order can be in the shopper's currency while the conversion action expects your store currency. Make sure the tag passes the currency code.
  • Static value: the action was created with a fixed value of 1, so every purchase is worth €1 to bidding.

Numbers that differ for legitimate reasons

Even with perfect tracking, Shopify and Google Ads won't match one to one. Google Ads attributes a conversion to the date of the click, Shopify counts orders on the order date. Google Ads counts only orders after an ad click within the conversion window, can include view-through and cross-device conversions, and models some conversions under consent mode. A 10–20% difference is normal. A factor of two isn't.

How to test

  1. Place a test order with a 100% discount code or a cheap product.
  2. Within a few hours, look at the action's status and the diagnostics tab in Google Ads.
  3. Tag Assistant often can't see events inside Shopify's checkout sandbox, so don't treat its silence as proof that nothing fired.
  4. Turn on enhanced conversions. The Google & YouTube app supports it, and it recovers part of the conversions lost to cookies and devices.
