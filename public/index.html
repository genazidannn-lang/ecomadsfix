How I work

No long forms. One chat on Telegram.

  1. You send the notice

    A screenshot of the issue in Merchant Center or Google Ads, plus your store URL.

  2. I tell you the likely cause

    I go through the store and policies the way a reviewer would. This first look is free.

  3. We fix it

    Either I make the changes with access to your account, or you get a precise list for your developer. The price is agreed after I've seen the account.

  4. One review request

    Only when everything is done. If Google still says no, we look at what's left before trying again.

What I won't do

Promise reinstatement

Google decides every review. Anyone who guarantees approval or a date is guessing. What I can promise is that you won't burn a review on a store that obviously isn't ready.

Open a new account to get around a suspension

Google treats it as circumventing its systems. That usually ends with every linked account suspended, including the one you could still have saved.
